# Phase 0: Centralized Deceased Notification System – Informed Discovery

## Round 0: Domain Research Summary
- **Industry Insight**: Bereavement is often the least digitized process in banking, leading to high fraud risk (1 in 5 cases).
- **Standards**: Mandatory certified documentation (Death Certificates, Letters Testamentary) is the "gold standard" for verification.
- **Operational Need**: Immediate freezing of sole-owned accounts is the standard protective action across financial institutions.

## Discovery Log
- **Round 4**: **Discovery Synthesis confirmed**. User validated the single-point-of-notification goal and broadcast strategy.

## Main Purpose
**Compassionate Service & Risk Mitigation**: Modernizing the enterprise bereavement process to prioritize customer experience. By centralizing reporting through a hybrid hub, we ensure intra-day data parity while requiring grieving families to notify the organization only once. (Exhaustive discovery: 4/5 rounds complete).

## Project Description (Draft for Sign-off)
The Centralized Deceased Notification System (CDN) is an enterprise-wide "Single Point of Notification" hub designed to modernize the bereavement lifecycle with compassion and security. It acts as an authoritative Broadcaster, accepting hybrid intake from manual support portals and automated LOB pushes to ensure intra-day status synchronization across all systems. By decoupling the notification from the execution, the CDN allows individual LOBs to decide on asset freezing or account closure while ensuring full process integrity through mandatory feedback loops and coordinates rollbacks via "Deceased in Error" broadcasts.
